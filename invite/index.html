A friend thinks you would like Nutovia.

Food packets are written to sell, not to inform. Sugar hides under a dozen names, and the list that matters is in tiny print on the back. Nutovia reads it for you: point your phone at any packaged food and, in a second, you get two scores out of 100, one for the product and one for you, with a better option from the same shelf. A slightly better choice every shop is how a family stays healthy.

The core features are free, and they stay free.

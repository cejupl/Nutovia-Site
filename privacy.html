Nutovia Privacy Policy

Version 2 - Last updated: August 12, 2026

Nutovia is a food-scanner app that helps families understand what is in packaged food. This policy explains what data we handle, why, where it lives, and the choices you have. The short version: your data belongs to your household, we show no ads, we never sell data, and health information is collected only for scoring products for you - with your explicit permission.

Who we are

Nutovia is operated by Cenovya Labs LLC, a company registered in the United States, which is the data controller for the personal data described here. For anything in this policy: privacy@nutovia.app.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address (and name, if your sign-in provider shares it) when you sign in with Apple, Google, or email.
  • Family profiles: the names or nicknames, food preferences, goals, and allergen restrictions you enter for members of your household - collected only after you give the permission described below, used only to score products for your family, and visible only to your household.
  • Your permission record: when you agree to the health-data permission in the app, we store when you agreed and which version of the wording you saw, so your consent is provable and withdrawable.
  • Scan history: the products you scan, their scores, favourites, and when you scanned them - so you can revisit them in History.
  • Store selections: the store chains you choose, used to rank alternatives.
  • Usage statistics: anonymous event counts and timings (for example, "a scan succeeded", "onboarding step completed") with your household id, the app version, and platform. These never contain product names, search terms, allergens, diets, goals, or any free text - the app is built so they cannot. Kept for 90 days, then deleted.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, a technical report is sent to our error-reporting service. It is stripped before sending: no email address, no IP address, no request contents - your account appears only as a random identifier so we can tell how many people a bug affects.

Health information and your permission

Allergies and some food preferences are health information. We ask for your explicit permission during setup, before any of it is collected, and we do not collect it without that permission. It is used for exactly one thing: scoring products for your household. It is never sold, never shared with brands, and never used for advertising.

You can withdraw this permission at any time by deleting your account (Settings → Account). There is no state in which Nutovia holds health information it no longer has permission for - withdrawing and deleting are the same act, and the app tells you so before anything happens.

Camera, photos, and location

  • Camera is used to read barcodes and photograph nutrition labels. Label photos are processed on your device; the extracted text may be sent to our servers for interpretation. Photos themselves are not uploaded or stored by Nutovia.
  • Location is used only when you tap "Detect the store I'm in", only while the app is in use, and only to find a nearby store name. To do that, your coordinates are sent from your device to OpenStreetMap's Overpass service (a community map database in Germany) - along with your IP address, as with any direct request, but never your identity, profiles, or history. Nutovia does not store your location.

How we use AI

When a product is missing from our databases, Nutovia may send the product's barcode, name, category, and label text to Anthropic (the AI service behind these features) to research publicly available nutrition information. When you ask for personalized alternatives, the dietary preferences, allergens, and goals of the active profile are included so suggestions fit that person - without any name, email address, or account identifier. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, these requests are not used to train their models. AI-sourced results are clearly marked in the app and are never trusted for allergen verification - only a product's real ingredients list can clear an allergen.

Where your data lives

Your household's data is stored in the European Union (London region) on Supabase, with access controls that ensure each household can only read and write its own data. Crash reports are stored in the EU as well. Product information you help identify (for example, a confirmed label) may be added, without any personal information, to the shared product catalog that benefits all users.

A few lookups happen directly from your phone rather than through our servers: barcode queries to Open Food Facts (a non-profit product database in France), store detection via OpenStreetMap's Overpass service, and store-name search via Photon. Each of these sees your IP address and the query itself (a barcode, coordinates, or a store name you typed) - but never your identity, profiles, or history.

Cenovya Labs LLC is a United States company. Like any US company, it can be subject to lawful requests from US authorities regardless of where data is hosted. We review every request we receive and disclose only what we are legally required to.

How long we keep things

  • Profiles, scans, stores: for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account removes all of it, immediately and permanently.
  • Usage statistics: 90 days.
  • Permission records: for as long as your account exists; deleted with the account.
  • Anonymous catalog contributions (nutrition facts with no personal information) may be retained for everyone's benefit.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can access, export, correct, and delete your data - the first three from inside the app, deletion in one step from Settings. If you are in the UK or EU, you additionally have the rights set out in the UK/EU GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent), and the right to complain to a supervisory authority - in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). If you are in a US state with a consumer privacy law, you have the rights it provides, including access, correction, and deletion; we honour these for everyone, and we do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising trackers.
  • No selling or renting of personal data, ever.
  • No third-party analytics - usage statistics stay on our own servers.
  • No brand can pay to change a score, a ranking, or a suggestion.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings → "Export my data" gives you a complete copy of your household's data at any time.
  • Correction: profiles and preferences can be edited or removed in the app at any time.

Data deletion

You can delete your account and all household data directly in the app: Settings → Account → Delete my account. Deletion withdraws your health-data permission and is immediate and permanent - your account, family profiles, and scan history are removed.

If you can no longer access the app, email privacy@nutovia.app from the email address associated with your account with the subject "Delete my Nutovia account" and we will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Children

Nutovia is not directed at children, and accounts are created by adults. Family profiles for children are entered and controlled entirely by the account holder - a parent or guardian - as part of their own household's data.

Changes

This policy is versioned. If it changes materially, we will note it in the app and update the version and date above; the permission you gave in the app is likewise versioned, and a material change to what it covers means we ask again rather than assume.

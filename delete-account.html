Delete your Nutovia account and data

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Nutovia account, published by Cenovya Labs LLC, and what happens to your data when you do.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Nutovia app and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to Account.
  4. Tap Delete my account.
  5. Confirm by tapping Delete everything.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It also withdraws your permission for Nutovia to hold health-related information, such as the diets and allergens in your household profiles. You are signed out as soon as it completes.

If you can no longer open the app

Email privacy@nutovia.app from the email address associated with your account, with the subject "Delete my Nutovia account". We will verify that the request comes from the account holder, delete the account within 30 days, and confirm by reply.

Delete some of your data without deleting your account

You do not have to close your account to remove data from it:

  • Household profiles, along with their diets, allergens and goals, can be edited or removed at any time in the app.
  • Saved stores can be removed individually from My Stores.
  • For anything else, email privacy@nutovia.app describing what you want removed. We respond within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Data What happens
Your account and sign-in identity Deleted immediately.
Household and family profiles, diets, allergens, goals Deleted immediately.
Scan and search history, saved stores, preferences Deleted immediately.
Permission records for health-data consent Deleted with the account.
Anonymous usage statistics Not linked to you by name or email, and automatically deleted 90 days after collection.
Corrections and reports you submitted Your submission records are deleted with your account. Where a correction was already accepted, the corrected product information stays in the shared food catalogue: it describes a product, not a person, and carries no link to you.
Purchase and billing records Held by Apple or Google, not by us. Cancel a subscription through the store that sold it; we cannot delete records that tax law requires those stores to retain.
Encrypted backups Removed as backups age out, within 30 days.

Questions

Write to privacy@nutovia.app. Our full Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why.

Questions

Questions, answered.

The ones worth asking first, in the order they matter: who pays for this, what costs nothing, how the number is built, and where it stops. Where the answer is a limitation, it says so.

Independence

Money, and who it comes from

Can a brand pay for a better score?

No. There is no mechanism for it, because there is nobody to pay. Nutovia takes no advertising, no sponsorship and no brand money of any kind. Every score is built from the product's own label and the same rules applied to everything else on the shelf.

Then how does Nutovia make money?

From the people who use it. Scanning and scores are free; Nutovia+ is a paid membership for the proactive tools - searching the whole catalogue, several family profiles, store matching. That is the entire business model, and it is why the only side we are on is yours.

Do you sell my data?

No. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or build a business on it. What you scan and who is in your household is health information, and it is treated that way. The Privacy Policy is the long version.

What are you actually trying to do?

Help our community eat better. That is the goal the rest of this follows from - if it ever conflicted with making more money, the goal wins, which is exactly the choice that staying independent keeps open.

Free

What you get without paying

What is free, forever?

Scanning any product, both of its scores and every reason behind them, healthier alternatives for anything you scan, reading a label with the camera, one profile, your fifty most recent products, searching your own history, and exporting your data. A score nobody can read is not a free tier, it is a demo.

What does Nutovia+ add?

The proactive side: searching and browsing the whole catalogue before you buy, up to seven family profiles, My Stores with store matching, your full history kept forever, and on-demand AI research when the catalogue comes up short. Scanning what is already in your hand stays free, always.

Why is search paid but scanning free?

Scanning answers "is the thing in my hand any good?" - the honest core, and never behind a paywall. Browsing the catalogue before anything reaches the trolley is a shopping tool rather than an answer, and it is what the membership sells.

Do I need an account?

Yes, because your profiles, history and stores follow you between devices and have to live somewhere. You can delete the account and everything in it at any time, from Settings or from this page.

Scores

The number, and what it means

Why do I see two scores?

One is the product's own - a neutral 0-100 that is the same for everybody. The other is yours, which takes the first and applies your household's allergens, diets, goals and avoid lists. A product can be excellent in general and wrong for you specifically, and one number cannot say both.

Where does the score come from?

The label, and nothing else. It starts from the Nutri-Score 2023 model and adds what that model leaves out - how processed a food is, and certain additives. How scoring works shows two real products end to end, including what it can still get wrong.

A score looks wrong to me. What now?

Tell us - there is a "report a data issue" link on every product page. Usually it means the underlying label data is wrong or incomplete rather than the maths, and that is fixable. We would rather hear it than have you quietly stop trusting the number.

Do scores change over time?

Only in planned, announced batches, or when a product's own label data changes or improves. A product you scanned last month scores the same today, and when the model itself changes we say so before it happens.

Allergens

Safety, and its limits

Can I rely on Nutovia for a serious allergy?

No - and no app should ask you to. Nutovia reads the ingredient list it holds, which can be out of date, incomplete, or missing entirely for a product. It is a good first filter and a bad last word. Always read the packet for anything that could send someone to hospital.

What does "traces" mean here?

May-contain warnings, which are a manufacturer's caution rather than an ingredient. Each profile chooses whether those block a product, because households genuinely differ on it - some avoid every trace warning, others eat them daily.

Why do Halal and Kosher say "inferred"?

Because that is what they are. We read the ingredients for things that would rule a product out, which is not the same as certification by an authority. If certification matters to you, the certificate on the packet is the answer, not us.

What about additives?

Flagged ones are shown with a plain-English fact and a source, grouped as restricted somewhere, worth watching, or reviewed and fine. They are deliberately informational and never part of the score - a regulatory fact is not a nutrition measurement.

Data

Where the products come from

Where does the product data come from?

Open Food Facts, a public collaborative database, plus the USDA's branded food data, mirrored and cleaned on our side. Roughly 850,000 products across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Ireland.

Why is a product missing, or missing its nutrition?

Because nobody has added it yet, or added it without the label. That is the honest cost of open data. Scanning an unknown product queues it for a background fill, which is free for everyone; Nutovia+ can research one on demand instead of waiting.

Which countries does it work in?

The United States, the United Kingdom, Canada and Ireland today. A barcode from elsewhere often still resolves, but the catalogue, the stores and the retailer links are built for those four.

Does it work offline?

Partly. Products you have already scanned stay readable, and the app tells you plainly when it cannot reach the network rather than blaming your connection for a problem at our end.
